# Profile revamp

Run once at setup, again when the goal changes (new job, new service). Takes two sessions: interview, then drafting.

## Session 1 — interview
Read `cv/` first. Then ask these questions **one block at a time**, wait for answers, do not draft yet.

Block A — goal
1. What should LinkedIn do for you in the next 6 months: a job, clients, or visibility? Pick one primary.
2. Who are the 2–3 kinds of people who should read your profile and think "that's who we need"? Be concrete (e.g. "CTO of a 50-person fintech", "recruiter at a Luxembourg bank").

Block B — proof
3. What are the three things you have done that you are proudest of, with a number each (users, latency, money, team size, time saved)?
4. Which of those numbers can you state publicly? Which clients can you name?

Block C — themes
5. What do people come to you for? What do you explain most often?
6. What do you believe about your field that half your peers would disagree with?
7. What would you never want to be hired for again?

Block D — voice
8. Paste three LinkedIn posts by anyone whose style you like. One line on why.
9. List five words or phrases you hate seeing on LinkedIn.
10. Primary language for posts. Second language and when.

Write the answers into `identity/positioning.md` (goal, audience, themes, proof points) and `identity/voice.md` (tone, never/always lists, reference posts). Mark anything you inferred with `(inferred)`.

## Session 2 — draft
Produce, in `identity/positioning.md` under "Approved profile copy", with `status: awaiting owner edits`:

**Headline** — 2 options, max 220 characters each. Formula: role | the outcome you produce for whom | 2–3 proof nouns. Not a list of job titles.

**About** — 2 options, 150–220 words each. Structure:
1. The problem your buyer has (one sentence, no "I")
2. What you do about it, present tense, with one concrete current example
3. Track record in one paragraph, oldest to newest or newest to oldest, with the public numbers
4. How you work (your method in 2–3 sentences, this is what differentiates)
5. What you are open to, stated plainly
6. Location, languages, one link

**Experience bullets** — for the last 3 roles, 3 bullets each: verb, what, outcome with number. Rewrite from `cv/`, never invent.

**Banner brief** — one line describing a banner image a designer or Canva template can make: text to show, colours, no stock photos of handshakes.

Finish with a checklist for the owner:
- [ ] pick headline option
- [ ] edit About, confirm every number
- [ ] apply on LinkedIn: headline, About, experience, banner, profile photo (recent, face fills 60% of frame, plain background)
- [ ] turn on "Open to" (work or providing services) matching the goal
- [ ] set a custom URL
- [ ] add 5 skills that match the three themes, remove the rest

Say which files changed.
