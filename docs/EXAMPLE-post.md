# the-pilot-is-not-the-project

## Hook
The pilot took three weeks. Getting operations to depend on it took five months.

## Body
Same codebase. Same team. The difference was everything the pilot was allowed to skip.

Retries on a flaky upstream. Idempotency when the queue redelivers. A dashboard the night shift could read. An on-call rota. A rollback that someone had actually run.

None of it is interesting. All of it is the project.

I now put the boring list on the wall on day one and estimate from it, not from the demo.

## Close
If your pilot has no runbook, it is a demo.
