# cv/

Put here everything Claude may treat as a **fact** about you:

- `cv.md` — your CV in markdown (export from LinkedIn PDF, paste, done)
- `projects.md` — one section per project: what, for whom, stack, your role, outcome, numbers you may state publicly
- `public-claims.md` — the short list of claims you are willing to defend in public ("1M downloads", "cut p95 from 800ms to 120ms"), each with where the number comes from

Rule: if it is not in this folder, Claude marks it `(inferred)` and you must confirm before it goes out.
Client-confidential detail stays out. Describe the system, not the client's internals.
